Where do I get my students' photos?

At UQ: log in to portal.my.uq.edu.au, choose My courses, pick your course, open the Student headshots tab under Course Information, and press Download .zip. Upload that zip here as it is. Only Course Coordinators can do this.

Elsewhere: most universities offer a photo roster through their learning or student system. Name each photo like Smith, Jane (12345678).jpg, then choose the folder or a zip of it.

Students with no photo on file are not in the download. Once the class is in, open Students to add them.
